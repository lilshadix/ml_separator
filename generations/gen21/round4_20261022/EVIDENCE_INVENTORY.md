## 1. What changes the round-4 design (verified, with the consequence)

1. **The strain corpus and the gen21 corpus are the same file.**
   - The `dataset.parquet` under `D:/ml_separator_gh/dataset with 3D structures/`, read by gen13 `paths.py`, has the git blob of strain's `collaborator_update/dataset.parquet`.
   - **Consequence:** any strain-trained supervised weights or OOF predictions are contaminated by construction. Encoders must be trained fold-locally, or start from a target-free initialisation.
   - No model weights of any kind exist at `025f808` anyway: no .pt, .cbm or .pkl files.
2. **The strain score is a different task.**
   - Strain is zero-shot leave-extractant-out with no measured supports, scores both-measured pairs inside a *binned* composition key, and uses ensemble OOF R².
   - Its numbers (0.1207 vs 0.1051; +0.0156) cannot be compared with gen21 B0/B1/B2 (about 0.42–0.45), and they cannot be reproduced locally because the OOF artifacts are absent.
   - The PASS rule was sign-only, with no interval. The 444 "fresh" pairs come from the same database after QC relaxation, and they are consumed.
3. **The strain "encoder shape" is not 3D-only.** The DistanceNet head also receives the full 2D tabular block and metal identity. A random-encoder or shuffled-geometry control must therefore keep the tabular fusion and the metal embedding; otherwise 3D information is confounded with head capacity.
4. **Geometry inputs exist locally, but at the extractant level only.**
   - `dataset with 3D structures/accepted_geometries.csv` and `features/vietoris_rips_inputs.npz`: 1,155 single-conformer GFN2-xTB Ln(III) complexes, 177 SMILES, 14 Ln.
   - All 229 C3 cells have at least one geometry; 191 / 229 have one for every metal.
   - Only **60 / 229** keep composition constant across their metals.
   - **Consequence:** cross-metal energy or strain features are composition-confounded except on that subset (gen15's naive slope collapses under composition control). Any geometry slot must keep the full 229-cell denominator with a B1 fallback.
5. **An(III) data exists locally with provenance.**
   - `dataset_all_metals/clean/master_clean.parquet` holds 2,067 An(III) rows: Am 1,775, Cm 151, Pu(III) 118, Cf 23. Only about 1,157 of them fall on 22 of the 79 C3 extractants (28 DOIs).
   - These rows come from the same SAFE records and share Eu rows and publications with the Ln cells. Exclusions must be applied over the Ln ∪ An union before any fit.
   - There is no An adjacent-curve target. Am/Cm is the only adjacent-like pair: 35 pairs on 12 extractants in strain.
   - Gen11 and gen19 pooled all An oxidation states. An An(III)-only, Ln-log-SF-scored arm is untried, but its support is small.
6. **Electronic features: generic forms are already tried; ligand-conditioned interactions are not.**
   - n_f = Z − 57 has a constant adjacent difference, so it cannot change a strict-adjacent log SF prediction beyond an intercept.
   - Already tried: tetrad and gd_break as global bases, f count, oxidation state, effective charge, and strain's mphys block (−0.0044).
   - **Untried:** donor-class × Gd / Nd amplitude interactions inside the k = 3 residual. gen13 D7's in-sample direction is Gd −0.127 outside sc009 vs −0.025 inside; Nd −0.109 vs −0.007. These must be re-estimated within outer training.
   - Strain's `half_shell` flags Eu, Gd, Yb and Lu; it is not a Gd term.
7. **R14 = a single Gd amplitude, fitted on outer training with inner-selected shrinkage, but its grid had no β = 0 endpoint.** B1 adds it (λ = ∞). R14 and R23 share the same fitted coefficient (mean −0.094 BP); R23 differs only in its reads. The Gd term is a series / read-set prior by construction: one shared coefficient on a read-set-and-position feature.
8. **Effective cluster counts are worse than the documents say.**
   - Chemotypes: Kish about 3.85 on pairs.
   - Chemotype ∪ publication components (14): Kish 2.68.
   - Adding gen19 copy groups: 13 components, Kish 2.24, with one component holding about 59% of pairs.
   - **Consequence:** inferential intervals on the declared clustering are fragile, and power on this corpus will be low. Effect-injection power analysis is required before any confirmation design.
