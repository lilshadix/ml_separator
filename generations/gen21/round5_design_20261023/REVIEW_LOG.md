folder: `README.md`, `RESEARCH_IDEAS.md` (RI), `CLAUDE_CODE_PROMPT.md` (brief) and `DATA_REQUESTS.md` (DR). No other

- Where a finding's own fix and the verdict's fix differed, the verdict's fix was applied.
- Every number inserted comes from the sources the documents cite (EB, IR, I2, P1–P5, PC, R4). The probe-check
  corrections take precedence.

  round-4 seal coverage, the cache contents and their `seconds` field, `clustered_null_size.csv`, and a within-publication
